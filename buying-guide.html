THE QUICK BUYING GUIDE

Know what you're buying.
Before you pay.

“Scratch-and-dent” and “open-box” describe different kinds of discounted inventory. The exact condition and protections vary by retailer and by individual appliance.

1. Inspect the exact unit

Ask to see the appliance you will receive. Photograph or document visible dents, scratches, missing parts, and any damage near controls, seals, hinges, cords, or water connections. Ask for the model and serial number.

2. Get warranty details in writing

Ask whether the manufacturer's warranty remains active, whether the retailer provides a separate warranty, what parts and labor are covered, and who handles service calls.

3. Understand the return policy

Ask whether the sale is final, what happens if the appliance does not work on delivery, and whether restocking or return-pickup fees apply. Keep the receipt and written terms.

4. Calculate the full delivered cost

Confirm delivery, installation, required hoses or connectors, haul-away, stairs, and old-appliance removal. A low sticker price can change once all fees are included.

5. Confirm fit and hookups

Measure the opening, doorways, and route into the home. Check electrical, gas, water, venting, and clearance requirements against the manufacturer's specifications. Use a qualified installer where required.

This guide is general shopping information, not technical, legal, or safety advice. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and applicable local requirements.
