ABOUT THE DIRECTORY

Less searching.
Better questions.

ApplianceScout is an independent directory designed to help people discover scratch-and-dent and open-box appliance retailers around Greater Houston, Texas.

We organize publicly available retailer information into a searchable directory and link to retailer sources wherever possible. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any retailer listed on this website.

How listings are selected

We prioritize businesses whose own websites or published retailer materials indicate that they offer scratch-and-dent, open-box, or discounted appliances. Branch addresses and phone numbers may come from retailer contact pages or public business listings. A retailer's general scratch-and-dent offering does not guarantee that every branch has such items in stock.

What we do not guarantee

We do not guarantee current inventory, pricing, business hours, delivery availability, product condition, warranties, returns, or retailer quality. Contact the retailer directly and review all purchase terms before paying.

If you spot an incorrect address, phone number, closed store, or outdated source, please report it through our corrections page.
